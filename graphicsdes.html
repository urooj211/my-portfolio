Project Guidelines

Important notes for design and submission

Recommended Software

  • Digital Branding: Adobe Photoshop
  • Brochure & Flyer: Adobe Illustrator
  • YouTube, Facebook & Social Media Ads: Any software

Work Process

  • First design all the given tasks, then create the mockups.
  • In Social Media Ads, design 2 separate designs of each size.
  • For a new product (other than the midterm project), design Logo & Stationery as well.
  • Detailed guidelines are shared in the WhatsApp group.

Submission

  • Submit the original files of the project with JPEG files.
  • Also submit the Mockup JPEG files.
  • Put all files in one folder and submit it as a zip file.
